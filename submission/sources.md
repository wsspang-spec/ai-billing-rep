## Illustrative economics, not observed savings

200 locations x 3 invoices/month x 12 = 7,200 invoices/year. At $6/invoice reference cost: $43,200. At an assumed $500/location/month telecom spend: $1.2M/year. Hypothetical 30% processing-cost reduction = $12,960; hypothetical 2% spend reduction = $24,000. Combined gross opportunity = $36,960 before fees and implementation costs. Location counts, volumes, spend, and reduction rates are assumptions. Efficiency may free capacity rather than reduce payroll. Do not include these assumed reductions in measured demo results.
